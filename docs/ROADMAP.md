# Development roadmap

Deliver a playable improvement on each useful work cycle; repair regressions before broadening systems. Keep this plan adjustable from play evidence.

1. Native foundation / current slice: direct object taps walk, search and open choices on arrival; drag-scrolling inventory, journal, build and command lists; original underground visuals and audio; seeded persistent floors with connected stairs; two selectable colonists; line-of-sight exploration; timed search, exact inventory transfers and local construction; camp heat, warned incursions, draft/attack and retreat; traveler hook; saved progress; Android and Windows exports.
2. Colony life: visible per-colonist task queues chain movement, searching, exact hauling, building, rest and travel. Filtered stockpiles and ghost building designations give safe idle colonists physical hauling and construction work with exact reservations, cancellation recovery and direct-order priority. Persistent Haul/Build levels control claims. Injuries slow movement/work; treatment and wearable protection remain physical. Critical colonists collapse, must be carried tile by tile to a reserved bedroll and require one safely delivered physical medkit before acting again. Fatigue grows faster during activity, slows physical action, eases around nearby companions and automatically sends exhausted idle colonists to a free bedroll only when safe. Shared expedition events create bounded pair-specific memories, bonds and morale effects—including rescue memories—without a maintenance-heavy social menu. Cinder Waystation and its supply-gated survivor provide the first inhabited location and third colonist. Aim for 20–30 minutes of varied, readable play.
3. Places with history: transit has paired rail platforms, a central concourse, Cinder Waystation and inspectable history. The Old Mines add shaft galleries, richer industrial salvage and warned collapses. Limestone Caverns form rounded chambers with natural landmarks and a one-time spring choice. The Sunken Quarter now has civic avenues, city records and a one-use pump that permanently reclaims one of two physical salvage branches without blocking the main descent. Physical pack-up work lets expeditions dismantle temporary camps into exact floor salvage before moving on.
4. Pressure and expeditions: a coordinated retreat order gathers available colonists at connected stairs and makes an intentional group descent readable. Camp pressure exposes its live trend, and a buildable clatter beacon shows a local attraction radius, accelerates pressure and physically diverts nearby surface husks until destroyed. Husks combine lantern-lit line of sight, nearby work noise and close scent; blind burrowers instead feel active movement through walls and only detect still bodies at close range. Reusable tripwire alarms watch open chokepoints, identify the first crossing creature, pause for a fair response, ring as a temporary sound/vibration focus, then require physical reset. No invisible raid timers presented as simulation.
5. Deep-city campaign: rumors, conflicting accounts, surviving factions, navigational clues and a real resolution. The connected campaign now reaches Wallward Descent through Meridian, Cairn Reach and the independent settlement network. Standing, carried evidence, exact material commitments, persistent route choices, warned emergencies and non-blocking failure all alter later places. Replayable seeds and difficulty settings remain later campaign work.
   Wayfarer Commons is complete: the carried convoy tally opens resident, hidden or public reception. Clear the lane, recover a 6 kg jack and gather two fit carriers with exact shared materials at the roadstead. Interrupted work consumes nothing; the completed repair preserves the tally, opens physical supplies, grants route-specific recovery or shelter and connects the Underway road. Next prioritize a **mobile expedition overview** that identifies essential cargo owners and helps regroup colonists across persistent depths before extending the campaign again.
6. Release readiness: actual Android and Windows device testing, performance and battery profiling, accessible text/input, robust save migration/backups, onboarding and balance, original asset polish, licensing review, release signing, store-specific packaging. Store submission and money/account steps require the owner when genuinely necessary.

Quality gates for each change: load prior native saves; preserve item accounting and travel; test changed simulation behavior; launch the game and inspect representative layouts; export only a working revision. Record actual results and limitations, not completion by aspiration.
