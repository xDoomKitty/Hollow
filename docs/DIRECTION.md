# Hollow: the descent
Working title provisional. Owner direction, September 17, 2026.

Humanity was forced underground after the surface became a hostile wasteland. You direct a small colony through a connected, procedurally generated underground world. A passing traveler speaks of a safe city far below, then disappears. Whether that city is safe is a question the game earns over time.

Exploration and building are primary. Camps provide light, food, storage and tools, but their heat and concentrated resources draw surface creatures down through access routes. Give fair, visible warning and useful defensive/evacuation choices; mobility should feel like an expedition, not a periodic deletion of everything the player built. Floors stay persistent and can be revisited. Procedural generation follows the history of each place: shelters and utilities near the surface, transit infrastructure, mines, caves, inhabited or abandoned underground settlements deeper down.

Controls: select colonists, give contextual movement/work orders, inspect visible objects, draft and target enemies, pause to plan. Touch gets large buttons and camera drag; desktop gets pointer/keyboard controls. Inventory is spatial and selective, with real hauling, quantities, weight, containers, equipment and practiced skills rather than abstract global resources.

Godot 4.5.1 / GDScript / 2D Compatibility renderer. Native Android and Windows first. No Apple target. Original game art and sound; engine and dependency notices retained. Commercial sale is a future goal, not a claim that this prototype is ready for a store.

The assistant owns routine planning, code, original assets, testing, packaging and project continuity. The owner plays and gives direction from Android. Continue concrete development on a four-hour schedule; do not require the owner to manage tools or provide tasks each run. Do not spend money, create accounts, sign publishing agreements, or publish a store listing without the necessary specific owner action.

The existing privately hosted browser prototype remains a preserved predecessor. Native saves start a new underground world; do not promise that browser saves migrate into the new engine.

Latest owner control direction, September 20, 2026: lists must scroll by pressing and dragging their contents up or down. A single object tap should send the selected colonist to it and interact on arrival. Preserve deliberate item quantities and meaningful choices.
