# Hollow 0.68 — Direct interaction and drag scrolling

A playable Godot prototype of an underground colony expedition. The title is provisional.

Canonical native source: [xDoomKitty/Hollow](https://github.com/xDoomKitty/Hollow). The repository preserves the original development history. Start with `AGENTS.md`, `docs/DIRECTION.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md` and `docs/CHECKPOINT.md` before development. Private Android signing material is stored separately.

## Play

Android: open **Hollow-Android.apk** on a 64-bit ARM Android phone (Android 7 or later, OpenGL ES 3). It installs as Hollow and plays in landscape. This is a signed development APK for direct installation, not a store release. Android handles any installation permission for the download source.

Windows: extract **Hollow-Windows.zip** and run **Hollow.exe**. The 64-bit executable is unsigned and includes its game data. No engine installation is required to play.

Linux: extract **Hollow-Linux.zip**, allow **Hollow.x86_64** to execute if your archive tool discarded its executable flag, and launch it.

Tap Begin the descent. Select Ash or Iona, then tap ground to move or an object to walk over and interact. Tap the traveler to talk; tap Shelter supplies to walk there, search and open its inventory. Bedrolls order rest, spent alarms order reset, stairs order travel, and settlement or spring options open on arrival. The sidebar still offers explicit actions. Pause holds orders until Play. Drag the map to look around. Press anywhere on an inventory, journal, build list or command list and drag up or down to scroll; a short tap selects a row. Select an item on the left, choose a quantity, and Take. Select an item in the colonist's pack on the right to Store, Drop, or Use it. Transfers happen after the colonist walks to the container.

Turn **Queue** on to chain up to four waiting orders for the selected colonist. Numbered map markers and the side panel show what happens next. A useful opening chain is: move near the supplies, take 4 timber, take 2 scrap, then place a workbench. The queue restarts safely after loading; **Clear** removes current and waiting work without moving any items remotely.

Open **Build** in Colony Blueprint mode, choose a structure and tap a clear tile. Its ghost plan shows delivered materials; safe idle colonists reserve distinct loads, physically supply the recipe and then claim the construction work. Tap a blueprint to approach and review its materials, then construct or explicitly cancel it; delivered goods remain physical. Switch the Build menu to Selected Colonist when you want the original direct, pack-funded construction instead.

Temporary camps can move with the expedition. Select a completed camp structure and use its **Manage** sidebar action, then choose **Dismantle**; the selected colonist must reach it and spend time packing it down. Cancellation leaves the structure intact. A completed pack-up returns a useful portion of its recipe as a searched ground inventory that must be hauled normally. Supply chests must be empty first and stop accepting automatic deliveries as soon as pack-up is ordered.

Use **Retreat together** when it is time to abandon a level. Available colonists physically path to the lower stair and early arrivals wait for the others before the expedition crosses. Drafted, lost, off-floor, or manually busy colonists are named and left in place rather than teleported. Routine automatic colony work yields to the retreat. Separate packs, equipment, wounds, and item quantities remain with their owners, and a rally in progress resumes safely after loading.

Build a **Clatter beacon** to reshape a retreat instead of only waiting for impact. Its animated map rings show the exact local attraction radius, and the colonist panel reports the beacon count and live camp-pressure trend. Nearby surface husks turn away from colonists to reach and tear down the lure. The beacon makes enough noise to accelerate the next warning, costs 1 timber and 3 scrap, and can be dismantled for physical salvage if it survives.

Threats no longer share one omniscient pursuit rule. **Surface husks** see stationary colonists in lantern light when they have a clear line of sight, hear active work at shorter range even through a wall, catch close scent, and still prioritize a nearby clatter beacon. **Burrowers** are blind: light does not reveal the colony to them, but movement and work create vibrations they feel through walls over a wider radius. A motionless colonist is only detectable at close body-heat range. Visible labels and touch inspection report what each creature currently senses, so waiting, working, lighting a room, or making deliberate noise are tactical choices.

Build a **Supply chest** to organize camp. Open its inventory to turn idle hauling on or off and toggle each item type. When camp is safe, idle colonists physically collect accepted goods and carry them to the chest. They keep two rations and one medkit as a personal survival kit. Visible threats pause colony work, and any direct command immediately takes priority without deleting carried items or staged supplies.

Each colonist has separate **Haul** and **Build** priorities in the side panel. High priority claims suitable work before Normal; Off prevents that colonist from automatically taking the role without restricting direct orders. Turning an active automatic role Off safely releases the task. The work line distinguishes automatic orders and explains why a colonist is idle or paused.

Colonists now accumulate **fatigue** while awake, especially while moving or working. Tired people move and work more slowly, while staying within five tiles of another living colonist reduces fatigue gain. The colonist panel and inventory show both fatigue and nearby company. At 80% fatigue, an idle colonist automatically claims a free bedroll when the level is safe; visible threats prevent automatic sleep, and direct orders still take priority. Bedroll rest restores fatigue as well as health and injury severity.

Shared expedition events now become **relationship memories**. Searching, raising camp, discovering landmarks, surviving combat, recruiting Vale and descending together strengthen the specific colonists who were present; a death leaves a lasting loss for survivors on that floor. Each colonist keeps six recent readable memories and a persistent bond with each companion. Repeating one action type on the same depth cannot farm the system. Hopeful morale modestly improves work and fatigue resistance, while shaken morale slows work and makes exhaustion arrive faster. The side panel and inventory expose morale, nearby bonds and the latest memory without adding a social-management screen.

Critical wounds now create a **rescue emergency** instead of leaving a barely alive colonist walking normally. The injured colonist collapses and loses impossible work or combat orders. Select a healthy companion, tap the downed colonist and choose Rescue: the companion physically reaches them, carries them tile by tile to a free bedroll and reserves that bed. Once the floor is clear of visible threats and active warnings, tap the patient again to spend one medkit from the rescuer's own pack on timed tending. The patient returns wounded rather than fully healed, and both colonists remember the rescue. Carrying and bed assignment survive saving and loading.

Shelter supplies now include one **Reinforced coat**. Take it through the physical inventory and use **Equip coat** to protect a colonist; the coat reduces incoming damage by 35%. Health thresholds create bruised, wounded and critical injuries that slow movement and work. Medkits and bedroll rest restore health and reduce injury severity. Equipment persists in saves and returns to the recoverable pack if its wearer falls.
